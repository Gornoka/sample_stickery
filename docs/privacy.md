# Shinybox privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

Shinybox ("the app", package com.gornoka.stickers) is made by [full name], [contact e-mail]. This policy explains what happens to data when you use the app.

## Summary
Shinybox has no server, no user accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking. The developer receives no data from the app.

## Data stored on your phone
Your sticker packs, editor projects, settings, the links you add and, if you set them up, your Telegram and Discord bot details (bot token and name, your Telegram user ID and name, the Discord server's ID and name) are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Android's cloud backup is turned off for the app; a phone-to-phone transfer when you set up a new phone may copy them. Uninstalling the app deletes them, except files you exported (see below).

## When the app goes online
The app only connects over encrypted HTTPS, and only in these cases:
- **Links you add:** to download sticker packs, the app requests the HTTPS links you entered, when you import them and about once a day while you keep them subscribed. The server behind a link sees your IP address and the request, as with any download. It is run by whoever published the link, not by the developer.
- **Telegram (optional):** if you set up your own Telegram bot, the app sends the bot token, your Telegram user ID, and the stickers, emojis and names of the packs you upload to Telegram's Bot API (api.telegram.org). To find your user ID, it reads the latest messages sent to your bot. If you turn on automatic updates, packs that changed are uploaded again during the daily update. Telegram's privacy policy applies: https://telegram.org/privacy
- **Discord (optional):** if you set up your own Discord bot, the app sends the bot token, the server ID, and the images and names of the stickers and emojis you sync to Discord's API (discord.com). If you turn on automatic updates, changes are synced during the daily update. Discord's privacy policy applies: https://discord.com/privacy

## Sharing with other apps on your phone
When you add a pack to WhatsApp or Telegram, the app hands the pack's files to that app on your phone; WhatsApp reads them through the app's sticker provider. Exports for Signal, Discord and .zip files are saved where you choose; they stay there after you uninstall the app. What other apps do with the stickers is covered by their own privacy policies.

## Permissions
Internet (downloads and the optional bots) and "run at startup" (to keep the daily link update scheduled after a restart). Images and files come from the system pickers, the camera app, the share menu or the clipboard, only when you choose them; the app has no access to your gallery, camera or files otherwise. The app checks which supported messengers are installed, to show the right buttons; this information does not leave your phone.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights
The developer does not hold any of your data. To delete data, delete it in the app or uninstall it, and delete uploaded sets in Telegram or Discord. Under the GDPR you may complain to a data protection authority.

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
