Demo sticker packs for Sticker Shim: cats, dogs, Pip the magpie (the app's mascot), Christmas and animated snow days.

- `sticker-shim-demo.zip`: all five packs. *+ → Import .zip* in the app brings them in as editable own packs, in the
  groups **Pets** and **Winter** (with the subgroup **Christmas**).
- `demo-<name>.zip`: one pack each, imported as a read-only pack.
